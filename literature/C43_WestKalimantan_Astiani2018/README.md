**How the paper used the data:** Astiani: weekly water table at dammed canals on bare burnt peat (March-December 2016) with CO2 (February 2016 - February 2017). Nusantara: 3 piezometers in each of 3 oil palm blocks (250/500/750 m), read morning and afternoon, 1-30 September 2021, Kubu village. Herawati: Wajok Hilir canal blocks (abstract). Nahda: Limbung burnt peat, September-December 2023 (abstract).

- Nusantara's period is September 2021 (the earlier row said 2021/08-2021/10).
