# C29: Dosan and Dayun villages, Siak (your A32)

**Status:** No public data

**Data source:** [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1088/1755-1315/648/1/012029)

**Paper:** Basuki et al. 2021, IOP Conf. Ser.: Earth Environ. Sci. 648, https://doi.org/10.1088/1755-1315/648/1/012029 (Open; downloaded to paper/)

> Data availability: No data availability statement.

**How the paper used the data:** Groundwater table measured with a stick in shallow wells at 0, 10, 25 and 50 m from canals in natural forest, mixed agriculture, shrub/ex-burnt, Acacia regrowth and oil palm plots, December 2017 - May 2019 (monthly plotted); mean -55 cm (Dosan) and -66 cm (Dayun); subsidence January 2018 - August 2019.
