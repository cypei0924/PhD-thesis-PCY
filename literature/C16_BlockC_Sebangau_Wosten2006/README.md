**How the paper used the data:** Jaenicke 2010 calibrated and validated a SIMGRO groundwater model against a dipwell at a test site (2.323 S, 113.903 E; daily, within 0.10 m) and simulated daily groundwater-level rise from dam construction in the Sebangau catchment for 2006-2008 (about +20 cm in the dry season). Wosten 2006 modelled groundwater and flooding in the Air Hitam Laut watershed (Jambi), not Block C.

- The Jaenicke 2010 test-site dipwell is the long-term Hokkaido station later published by Sulaiman 2023 (B4).
- Most of this entry is modelling; measured series appear only as calibration data.
