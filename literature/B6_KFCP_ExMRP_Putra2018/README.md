# B6: KFCP dipwell network, ex-Mega Rice Project Blocks A and E, Central Kalimantan

**Status:** No public data

**Data source:** [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1088/1755-1315/149/1/012027)

**Paper:** Putra et al. 2018, IOP Conf. Ser.: Earth Environ. Sci. 149, https://doi.org/10.1088/1755-1315/149/1/012027 (Open; downloaded to paper/)

> Data availability: No data availability statement (IOP proceedings).

**Paper:** Putra et al. 2019, IOP Conf. Ser.: Earth Environ. Sci. 284, https://doi.org/10.1088/1755-1315/284/1/012021 (Open; downloaded to paper/)

> Data availability: No data availability statement.

**Paper:** Hikouei et al. 2023, Science of the Total Environment 857, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scitotenv.2022.159701 (See D12_ML_GWL_Hikouei2023)

> Data availability: See D12.

**How the paper used the data:** Putra 2018: monthly GWL (blow-straw method) from 300 dipwells of the Kalimantan Forests and Climate Partnership (KFCP) in northern Block A and southern Block E of the ex-Mega Rice Project, January 2010 - January 2013, compared with TRMM rainfall and MODIS hotspots to find a critical GWL for fire. Putra 2019 extended the analysis to 2010-2017 and estimated hydraulic conductivity from the 2010-2012 data.

- Dipwell coordinates are only shown on maps (Putra 2018, Fig. 1).
