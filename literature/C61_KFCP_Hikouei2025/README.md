# C61: KFCP dipwell network 2011-2019, Central Kalimantan (MODFLOW + XGBoost)

**Status:** No public data

**Data source:** [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gsd.2025.101413)

**Paper:** Hikouei et al. 2025, Groundwater for Sustainable Development 29, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gsd.2025.101413 (Abstract only (closed access or the publisher blocked the download); full text not read)

> Data availability: The authors do not have permission to share the data.

**How the paper used the data:** A MODFLOW groundwater model of a degraded peat dome calibrated on monthly manual readings from 265 dipwells (2011-2019); XGBoost explained the residuals.

- Extends B6 (KFCP, 300 dipwells 2010-2017) to 2019; same lead author as D12.

Full details: `../DATA_SUMMARY.md`. Papers go in `paper/` (git-ignored); run `../download_papers.py C61`.
