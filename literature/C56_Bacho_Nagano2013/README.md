# C56: Bacho (Narathiwat), To Daeng and Nakhon Si Thammarat, Thailand

**Status:** No public data

**Data source:** [Paper](https://doi.org/10.19189/001c.128481)

**Paper:** Nagano et al. 2013, Mires and Peat 11, https://doi.org/10.19189/001c.128481 (Open; downloaded to paper/)

> Data availability: No data statement.

**How the paper used the data:** Water table recorded at each monthly subsidence observation at Bacho 1-5 (6 29 N, 101 45 E) from July 1983 to January 2006 (WT -116 to -54 cm in the 1987 fires); HOBO U20 water level with continuous CO2 at Nakhon Si Thammarat 2006-2009; To Daeng (6 12 N, 101 56 E) undrained forest spot measurements.

- The longest WT record found for Thailand; per-point periods are tabulated in the paper.

Full details: `../DATA_SUMMARY.md`. Papers go in `paper/` (git-ignored); run `../download_papers.py C56`.
