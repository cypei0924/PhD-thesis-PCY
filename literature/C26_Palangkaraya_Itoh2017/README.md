# C26: Palangkaraya UF/DF/DB trenching chambers and hourly GWL

**Status:** No public data

**Data source:** [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scitotenv.2017.07.132)

**Paper:** Itoh et al. 2017, Science of the Total Environment 609, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scitotenv.2017.07.132 (Open; downloaded to paper/)

> Data availability: No data statement found in the accepted manuscript.

**How the paper used the data:** Hourly GWL (pressure sensor in PVC pipes) from January 2014 to December 2015 at UF (2.32 S, 113.90 E), DF (2.35 S, 114.04 E) and DB (2.34 S, 114.04 E), used to upscale peat decomposition: mean GWL -0.23/-0.39 m (UF), -0.55/-0.59 m (DF), -0.22/-0.62 m (DB) in 2014/2015; 2015 minima -1.43 to -1.62 m.

- Same three tower sites as A3 (whose files hold half-hourly GWL for the same period).

Full details: `../DATA_SUMMARY.md`. Papers go in `paper/` (git-ignored); run `../download_papers.py C26`.
