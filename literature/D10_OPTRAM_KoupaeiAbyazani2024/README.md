# D10: OPTRAM satellite WT estimates (your D3)

**Status:** Data already in this repository

**Data source:** [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1029/2024JG008116)

**Paper:** Koupaei-Abyazani et al. 2024, JGR Biogeosciences 129, https://doi.org/10.1029/2024JG008116 (Open; downloaded to paper/)

> Data availability: 'In situ water table data was used with permission from Takashi Hirano, Lulie Melling, Guan Xhuan Wong, and Kristell Hergoualc'h ... Data for the Indonesian sites are available in Hirano, 2023 (https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.22321129.v1). Data for the Malaysian sites are available in Melling & Wong, 2024 (https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.25299358.v1).' Code: github.com/koupsci/OPTRAM_code_updated.

**How the paper used the data:** Landsat OPTRAM moisture indices were related to in situ WT at IN-undrained (2.32 S, 113.90 E; 2015-2018), IN-drained (2.35 S, 114.04 E; 2013-2017), MA-undrained (1.4536 N, 111.1494 E; 2011-2014), MA-converted (2.1860 N, 111.8459 E; 2018-2019) and two Peruvian sites.

- The SE Asian in situ data are A4 (DailyGWL.xlsx) and A15 (Melling & Wong 2024), both downloaded.

Full details: `../DATA_SUMMARY.md`. Papers go in `paper/` (git-ignored); run `../download_papers.py D10`.
