# C18: Upper Sebangau forest and Air Hitam (Jambi) groundwater simulations

**Status:** No public data

**Data source:** [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.geoderma.2019.04.001)

**Paper:** Taufik et al. 2019, Geoderma 347, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.geoderma.2019.04.001 (Open; downloaded to paper/)

> Data availability: No data statement; observed groundwater depths used for inverse modelling are shown in Annex I.

**How the paper used the data:** SWAP simulations of daily groundwater table depth for 1980-2015 at the Sebangau Forest and Air Hitam (Jambi), with water-retention curves for fibric, hemic and sapric peat; the fibric curve was obtained by inverse modelling of observed groundwater depths (Annex I). Drought (80th-percentile threshold) and fire hazard were then assessed.

- The series in this paper are simulated; the observed calibration data (2000-2008 Sebangau, 2003-2004 Air Hitam per Apers Table B1) are only plotted.

Full details: `../DATA_SUMMARY.md`. Papers go in `paper/` (git-ignored); run `../download_papers.py C18`.
