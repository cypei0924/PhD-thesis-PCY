# C63: Canal block transect (5 dipwells, 20-220 m), Tebing Tinggi Island, Riau

**Status:** No public data

**Data source:** [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1051/matecconf/201927606003)

**Paper:** Sutikno et al. 2019, MATEC Web of Conferences 276, https://doi.org/10.1051/matecconf/201927606003 (Open access, but the publisher blocked the download; abstract only)

> Data availability: Not read.

**How the paper used the data:** GWL monitored in five dipwells at 20, 70, 120, 170 and 220 m perpendicular to a canal block; rewetting raised the water table up to about 170 m from the canal (abstract and secondary reports).
