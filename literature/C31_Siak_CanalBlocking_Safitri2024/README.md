# C31: Bunsur village (Sungai Apit, Siak) canal blocking transects

**Status:** No public data

**Data source:** [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1088/1755-1315/1315/1/012058)

**Paper:** Safitri et al. 2024, IOP Conf. Ser.: Earth Environ. Sci. 1315, https://doi.org/10.1088/1755-1315/1315/1/012058 (Open; downloaded to paper/)

> Data availability: No data availability statement.

**How the paper used the data:** Manual GWL (measuring stick) in wells 5, 100, 400 and 900 m from blocked and unblocked canals under oil palm, rubber, sago, shrub and paludiculture, April 2022 - March 2023; mean -26.7 cm near blocked vs -58.7 cm near unblocked canals.
