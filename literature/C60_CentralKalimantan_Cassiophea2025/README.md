# C60: Five IoT WTD stations (RePEAT, CIMTROP forest, Ruslan Canal, KHDTK, KM 16), Central Kalimantan

**Status:** No public data

**Data source:** [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1088/1755-1315/1542/1/012023)

**Paper:** Cassiophea et al. 2025, IOP Conf. Ser.: Earth Environ. Sci. 1542, https://doi.org/10.1088/1755-1315/1542/1/012023 (Open; downloaded to paper/)

> Data availability: No data availability statement; WTD is shown only in figures.

**How the paper used the data:** Custom IoT stations (pressure transducer in a 2-3 m perforated PVC well, solar power, LTE upload) recorded WTD at hourly to daily intervals from November 2024 to April 2025 at RePEAT (rehabilitated reference), CIMTROP Lab Forest (little disturbed), Ruslan Canal (Sebangau NP, canal-blocked), KHDTK (forest under restoration) and KM 16 (restored shrubland); monthly manual readings with a water-level meter checked the sensors. Daily WTD was correlated with NASA POWER temperature, humidity and rainfall and with monthly soil samples (Ksat, bulk density, porosity): WTD responded weakly to daily rain, KM 16 stayed ponded (+20 to +40 cm) and KHDTK ranged from -40 to +25 cm.
