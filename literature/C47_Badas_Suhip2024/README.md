# C47: Badas peat dome piezometers, Brunei

**Status:** No public data

**Data source:** [Paper](https://doi.org/10.19189/map.2023.cm.sc.2332104)

**Paper:** Suhip et al. 2024, Mires and Peat 31, https://doi.org/10.19189/map.2023.cm.sc.2332104 (Open; downloaded to paper/)

> Data availability: No data availability statement.

**How the paper used the data:** 13 piezometers (peat and underlying sand, in pairs on two transects plus a lagoon well) with Solinst Leveloggers at 20-min intervals, August 2020 - January 2021, with a tipping-bucket gauge, topographic and seismic surveys; max/min/median heads are tabulated.

- Same dome as A22 and your C12.

Full details: `../DATA_SUMMARY.md`. Papers go in `paper/` (git-ignored); run `../download_papers.py C47`.
