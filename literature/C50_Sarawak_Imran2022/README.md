# C50: Maludam NP: MPS, Alan Batu, Alan Bunga sites

**Status:** Embargoed / on request

**Data source:** [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1088/2515-7620/ac6295)

**Paper:** Imran et al. 2022, Environmental Research Communications 4, https://doi.org/10.1088/2515-7620/ac6295 (Open; downloaded to paper/)

> Data availability: 'The data that support the findings of this study are available upon reasonable request from the authors.'

**How the paper used the data:** HOBO U20 loggers in two piezometers per site recorded every 30 min (processed to monthly means), February 2011 - December 2020, with peat surface level and rainfall; WT and surface fell to their lowest in 2019.

- Coordinates: MPS 1 25 51.2 N, 111 07 52.0 E; Alan Batu 1 27 12.7 N, 111 08 57.4 E; Alan Bunga 1 27 47.9 N, 111 09 29.0 E.

Full details: `../DATA_SUMMARY.md`. Papers go in `paper/` (git-ignored); run `../download_papers.py C50`.
