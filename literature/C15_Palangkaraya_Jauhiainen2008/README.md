# C15: Palangkaraya drained forest and burnt site before/after canal dams

**Status:** No public data

**Data source:** [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1890/07-2038.1)

**Paper:** Jauhiainen et al. 2008, Ecology 89, https://doi.org/10.1890/07-2038.1 (Abstract only (closed access or the publisher blocked the download); full text not read)

> Data availability: Not read (closed).

**How the paper used the data:** CO2 and CH4 fluxes and water tables in a drained peat swamp forest and a deforested burnt area before and after dams were built in drainage canals (2004-2007); annual minimum water tables were higher after damming (abstract).

- Coordinates from Apers 2022 Table B1: burnt site 2.3381 S, 114.0297 E; drained forest 2.345 S, 114.0367 E.
