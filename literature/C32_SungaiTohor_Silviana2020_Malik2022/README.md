# C32: Sungai Tohor (Tebing Tinggi Island) rewetting

**Status:** No public data

**Data source:** [Silviana 2020](https://doi.org/10.1088/1757-899X/796/1/012041)

**Paper:** Silviana et al. 2020, IOP Conf. Ser.: Mater. Sci. Eng. 796, https://doi.org/10.1088/1757-899X/796/1/012041 (Open; downloaded to paper/)

> Data availability: No data statement.

**Paper:** Malik et al. 2022, IOP Conf. Ser.: Earth Environ. Sci. 1041, https://doi.org/10.1088/1755-1315/1041/1/012047 (Open; downloaded to paper/)

> Data availability: No data statement.

**How the paper used the data:** Silviana: 66 monitoring wells (33 unburnt, 33 burnt), read twice a month, February-June 2019, plus the SESAME station. Malik: daily levels from one logger for a year in Sungai Tohor and one in Kundur, reported as elevations (8.78-9.72 m), not depths.
