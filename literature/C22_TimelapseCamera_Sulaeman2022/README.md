# C22: Time-lapse camera peat motion and WT, 4 land covers (South Sumatra, Central Kalimantan)

**Status:** No public data

**Data source:** [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1088/1755-1315/1025/1/012011)

**Paper:** Sulaeman et al. 2022, IOP Conf. Ser.: Earth Environ. Sci. 1025, https://doi.org/10.1088/1755-1315/1025/1/012011 (Open; downloaded to paper/)

> Data availability: No data availability statement.

**How the paper used the data:** Wingscapes time-lapse cameras photographed a subsidence pole and an adjacent dipwell every 3 hours for about a year at four sites: forest and oil palm in a South Sumatra peat hydrological unit, coconut (Kahayan-Sebangau PHU) and shrub (Kapuas-Barito PHU) in Central Kalimantan; peat motion was related to WTD (R2 0.74-0.95).
