# C52: Sebungan and Sabaju oil palm estates, Sarawak

**Status:** No public data

**Data source:** [Paper](https://doi.org/10.5194/bg-15-7435-2018)

**Paper:** Cook et al. 2018, Biogeosciences 15, https://doi.org/10.5194/bg-15-7435-2018 (Open; downloaded to paper/)

> Data availability: 'Data are available in Cook (2018).' (PhD thesis)

**How the paper used the data:** Water-table depth by dipmeter in a cluster of three dipwells per estate at each sampling visit (about weekly), August 2015 - September 2016, at Sebungan and Sabaju 1, 3 and 4; mean, min, max and % time deeper than 60 cm per site are reported; WT controlled DOC export.

- Same estates as your A12. The supplement (saved in paper/) has rating curves and flux tables, not WT.

Full details: `../DATA_SUMMARY.md`. Papers go in `paper/` (git-ignored); run `../download_papers.py C52`.
