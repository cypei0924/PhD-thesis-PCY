# C64: Tangkit Baru and Pematang Rahim villages, Jambi: submersible sensors vs manual

**Status:** No public data

**Data source:** [Paper](https://doi.org/10.29244/j-siltrop.15.01.65-69)

**Paper:** Putra et al. 2024, Journal of Tropical Silviculture 15, https://doi.org/10.29244/j-siltrop.15.01.65-69 (Open; downloaded to paper/)

> Data availability: No data availability statement; GWL readings are shown in figures and on a Telkom IoT dashboard.

**How the paper used the data:** September - November 2023: one IoT station per village (submersible pressure sensor read by an ESP32 microcontroller, sent to the Telkom IoT server) in a dipwell, compared with manual measuring-stick readings in the same dipwell; GPM daily rainfall from NASA. No significant difference between the methods (t-test, p > 0.05; offsets 0.1-1 cm); GWL rose with rain at Tangkit Baru but fell at Pematang Rahim.

- Tangkit Baru (Sungai Gelam, Muaro Jambi): drained pineapple farms with 11 canals 1-2 m deep. Pematang Rahim (Mendahara Ulu, Tanjung Jabung Timur): next to the Sungai Buluh peat protection forest of A17. Coordinates not given (map only).
