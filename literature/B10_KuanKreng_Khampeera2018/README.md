# B10: Kuan Kreng peat swamp (Bor Lor, Thale Noi), Nakhon Si Thammarat, Thailand

**Status:** No public data

**Data source:** [Paper](https://doi.org/10.48048/wjst.2018.2723)

**Paper:** Khampeera et al. 2018, Walailak Journal of Science and Technology 15, https://doi.org/10.48048/wjst.2018.2723 (Open; downloaded to paper/)

> Data availability: No data availability statement; water-level data came from the Pak Phanang Fire Control Station.

**How the paper used the data:** Drought indices (SPI, NDDI, standardised water-level index SWI and water-table level WTL) for the Kuan Kreng peat swamp (7 37'-8 26' N, 99 41'-100 22' E). Hydrological drought used monthly means from 50 water-level monitoring stations in the Bor Lor and Thale Noi non-hunting zones, focusing on 2010 (El Nino) and 2012; water levels fell below the surface for 4 months in 2012.
