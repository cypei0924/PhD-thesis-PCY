# C36: Acacia plantation CO2 transects, Sumatra (your A29)

**Status:** No public data

**Data source:** [Paper](https://doi.org/10.5194/bg-9-617-2012)

**Paper:** Jauhiainen et al. 2012, Biogeosciences 9, https://doi.org/10.5194/bg-9-617-2012 (Open; downloaded to paper/)

> Data availability: No data availability section (2012).

**How the paper used the data:** Water table monitored monthly or quarterly in perforated PVC tubes at the CO2 points along 8 transects (A-H, 700 m long, up to 28 km apart) on one Acacia plantation dome, April 2007 - April 2009; average WTD about 0.8 m; Table 2 gives WT statistics per transect.

- Your A29 is the same study.
- Transect coordinates are not given.
