# C40: Jabiren rubber plantation, Central Kalimantan

**Status:** No public data

**Data source:** [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scitotenv.2017.01.035)

**Paper:** Wakhid et al. 2017, Science of the Total Environment 581-582, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scitotenv.2017.01.035 (Open; downloaded to paper/)

> Data availability: No data statement in the accepted manuscript.

**How the paper used the data:** Hourly GWL (June 2014 - December 2015) and monthly chamber CO2 (December 2014 - December 2015) in an 8-year-old rubber plantation at 2 29 50 S, 114 11 20 E; annual soil respiration was computed from hourly GWL; minimum GWL -1.71 m in 2015 vs -1.35 m in 2014.


Full details: `../DATA_SUMMARY.md`. Papers go in `paper/` (git-ignored); run `../download_papers.py C40`.
