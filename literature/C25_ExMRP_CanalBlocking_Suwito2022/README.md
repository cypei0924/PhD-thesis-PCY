# C25: Ex-MRP Blocks E and B canal blocking, Central Kalimantan

**Status:** No public data

**Data source:** [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1088/1755-1315/1018/1/012027)

**Paper:** Suwito et al. 2022, IOP Conf. Ser.: Earth Environ. Sci. 1018, https://doi.org/10.1088/1755-1315/1018/1/012027 (Open; downloaded to paper/)

> Data availability: No data availability statement.

**How the paper used the data:** Spot water-level readings on 14 and 28 February 2020 (wettest month) and in August 2020 (driest) at six locations (Tuanan I, Tuanan II-Katunjung, Camp Release, Bagantung, Kanal Primer, Mantangai Hulu) under four conditions (large, medium, small and no canal block); values are tabulated in the paper.


Full details: `../DATA_SUMMARY.md`. Papers go in `paper/` (git-ignored); run `../download_papers.py C25`.
