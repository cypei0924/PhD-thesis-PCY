# B4: Sebangau (Palangkaraya UF) daily GWL 1993-2019, Central Kalimantan

**Status:** Embargoed / on request

**Data source:** [Paper (BRIN release planned)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-27393-x)

**Paper:** Sulaiman et al. 2023, Scientific Reports 13, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-27393-x (Open; downloaded to paper/)

> Data availability: 'The monthly in situ groundwater level and rainfall data sets will be publicly available two years after the completion of the collaborative Japan-Indonesia Collaboration Research of Tropical Peatland project through the National Research and Innovation Agency (BRIN).'

**How the paper used the data:** A monitoring station in Sebangau National Park (2.321002 S, 113.901161 E) recorded daily GWL with a pressure sensor from 1 September 1993 to 12 December 2019. GWL and rainfall anomalies were compared with Nino3.4, the Dipole Mode Index, SST and Ekman transport: GWL dropped 1.0-1.5 m in the 1997/98 and 2015 El Ninos and the 2019 positive IOD, and dry-season minima deepened from about -0.9 to -1.0 m before 2010 to -1.3 to -1.4 m after 2015. GWL is proposed as an alert indicator for El Nino and IOD+ events.

- Same location as the Palangkaraya UF tower (A3, A4), extending that record back to 1993.
- The statement promises monthly (not daily) data.
