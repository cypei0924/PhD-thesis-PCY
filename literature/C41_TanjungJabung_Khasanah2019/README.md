# C41: Smallholder mosaic, Tanjung Jabung Barat, Jambi

**Status:** No public data

**Data source:** [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11027-018-9803-2)

**Paper:** Khasanah & van Noordwijk 2019, Mitig. Adapt. Strateg. Glob. Change 24, https://doi.org/10.1007/s11027-018-9803-2 (Open; downloaded to paper/)

> Data availability: No data availability statement.

**How the paper used the data:** Subsidence every 6 months (November 2012 - May 2015) at four points per transect in a logged-over forest and four smallholder land uses; water-table depth at the points was related to subsidence rates.
